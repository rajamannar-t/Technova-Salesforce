# Technova-Salesforce
## Overview
Technova is a Salesforce-based Sales Management System designed to manage
leads, customers, contacts, opportunities, sales automation, security,
reports, and dashboards.

## Key Features
- Lead Management
- Account and Contact Management
- Opportunity Pipeline
- Formula Fields
- Validation Rules
- Record-Triggered Flows
- Duplicate Prevention
- Salesforce Security
- Reports and Dashboards

## Business Flow
Lead → Account/Contact → Opportunity → Sales Process → Closed Won/Lost

## Salesforce Concepts
Objects | Fields | Relationships | Flow | Validation Rules |
Formula Fields | Profiles | Permission Sets | Roles | OWD |
Sharing Rules | Field-Level Security | Reports | Dashboards

## Project Outcome
The application provides a centralized sales process and gives managers
visibility into pipeline, sales performance, lead sources, and lost-deal reasons.
